## Findings

### 1. Epic-1 context and the epic spec disagree about scope (spec-to-implementation reconciliation)

- **Source:** `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/epic-1-context.md:9-13` lists two stories for Epic 1 — Story 1.1 (tagging) **and** "Story 1.2: Close the sudoers NOPASSWD workaround (issue #160)" — and line 35 states "Story 1.1's tagging of the sudoers item is only final after Story 1.2 lands."
- **Contradicts:** `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md:64` and its FR Coverage Map, which explicitly scopes FR9 (the sudoers fix) **out** of BMad: *"FR9: No BMad story - already tracked directly via GitHub issue #160."* Epic 1 in `epics.md` is defined with exactly one story.
- **Corroborating evidence:** GitHub issue #160 is still `OPEN` (verified via `gh issue view 160`), and the docs still carry the sudoers item as `_Disposition: tracked to close_` — consistent with `epics.md`'s scope, but exactly the state `epic-1-context.md` said would make the tagging "not final."
- **Both documents were authored and committed together**, in the single commit `fbc93d8` — this is not two documents drifting apart over time; the inconsistency shipped in one commit.
- **Disposition:** accept-as-is for this epic's acceptance verdict — `epics.md` is the authoritative, human-reviewable planning artifact, its declared AC (tag present + issue number linked) is what Story 1.1 was built against, and that AC is fully met (see Findings #3). The stale "Story 1.2" reference in `epic-1-context.md` is a documentation defect, not a delivery gap.
- **Proposed fix-now action item:** reconcile `epic-1-context.md` — either remove the "Story 1.2" reference, or replace it with an explicit note that issue #160's resolution is tracked entirely outside BMad and is not a story of this epic. Left as-is, a future reader (human or agent) will see an "only final after Story 1.2 lands" caveat with no Story 1.2 anywhere in sprint tracking, and reasonably conclude Epic 1 was closed with unfinished work.
- **Process lesson:** whatever step compiles `epic-1-context.md` should reconcile against the epic-breakdown's own FR Coverage Map before including a story implied by the epic's narrative goal — the goal text ("...the one step that actually should close...gets resolved") pulled FR9 back in even though `epics.md` had already explicitly excluded it.
