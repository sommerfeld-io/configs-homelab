### Aggregate views

- **Architecture delta:** `configs-cloud` now depends on four homelab roles instead of three: `grafana-cloud/exporters` was added (`a998ab9`), and the fantasy-hockey role reaches into `ansible-roles-collection/docker` and `/bash` (`tasks/user.yml`). A change to a fleet role now changes the production droplet (D6).
- **Pattern divergence:** cross-repo includes use two styles: `provision.yml` and `deploy-fantasy-hockey.yml` use playbook-relative `../../../../configs-homelab/...`, `roles/fantasy-hockey/tasks/user.yml` uses `{{ role_path }}/../../../../../...` (needed because `include_role` resolves relative names from the playbook folder). Accepted; the reason is written in `user.yml`.
- **Duplication:** the deploy prompt is a hand copy of the `ubuntu_server` `ps1` in `configs-homelab/ansible/hosts.yml` (comment says keep in sync); the mount path has two sources (D8).
- **Size growth:** no file grew past a healthy size. The largest net additions are the moved data template (`fantasy-hockey.yml.j2`, counted as add plus delete because `git_evidence.py` does not follow renames), the Molecule `verify.yml` (+236) and `deployment.md` (+104).
- **Churn signal:** `docs/cloud/digital-ocean/replacement.md` was written, reviewed (18 findings), rewritten and then cut down to a short page in three commits (+189/-139). Most of the reviewed procedure was dropped after the drill showed what actually happens.
